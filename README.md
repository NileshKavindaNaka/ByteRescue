## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is ByteRescue safe to use?
**A:** Yes! ByteRescue is open-source software that only reads data from your drives. It never writes or modifies anything unless you specifically choose to recover a file.

### Q: Can I recover files from a USB drive?
**A:** Absolutely! ByteRescue supports USB drives, SD cards, and external hard drives.

### Q: How long does a scan take?
**A:** It depends on the size of the drive. A small 16GB USB stick may take 2–5 minutes, while a large 1TB hard drive could take 30–60 minutes.

### Q: Will recovery always work?
**A:** Unfortunately, no tool can guarantee 100% recovery. If the space has been overwritten by new files, recovery may not be possible. The sooner you attempt recovery, the better your chances.

### Q: Do I need to install anything else?
**A:** On most Windows systems, no. But if the app doesn't start, you'll need to install Python (free from python.org).

## 🌟 Tips for Best Results

1. **Act quickly**—the longer you wait, the lower your chances of recovery.
2. **Stop using the drive** immediately after you delete something important.
3. **Use a different drive** to save recovered files (to avoid overwriting the original data).
4. **Run a quick analysis first** to understand your storage layout before deep scanning.
